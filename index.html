⚡ Battle of Pokrovsk
October – December 2024 · Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine
Following the fall of Avdiivka (Feb 2024) and the Ocheretyne breakthrough (May), Russian forces accelerate their drive toward Pokrovsk — the critical rail junction and UAF logistics hub in Donetsk Oblast.
Three simultaneous Russian axes: northeast via Toretsk, directly west through Selydove, and south after the capture of Vuhledar (~1 Oct 2024). Ukraine races to establish defensive lines.
🇺🇦 UAFHold Pokrovsk — deny the rail junction and keep western supply lines open.
🇷🇺 ZVOCapture Pokrovsk and advance to the Dnipropetrovsk oblast border.
